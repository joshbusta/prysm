PrYSM

Providence Youth Student Movement

Our mission is to confront and end state, street, and interpersonal violence affecting the Southeast Asian youth, queer & trans youth of color, and survivors of state violence communities in Rhode Island.

PrYSM Documentary

We organize at the intersections of race, class, gender, and sexual orientation by centering youth, female, queer, and people of color leadership.
