Exciting news: Executive Director search

We’ve started our search for the next Executive Director of Providence Youth Student Movement. We’re searching for an experienced leader to drive forward our programs, campaigns, and strategic priorities in ending cycles of violence in our communities. This is an exciting opportunity to lead a team of staff, youth, and community members in building grassroots power and liberation for all.

We are seeking a candidate:

  • Who is committed to empowering the leadership of youth of color and Southeast Asian refugees in undoing the school to prison to deportation pipeline
  • With deep knowledge and relationships of our communities and organizing movements
  • Who will execute core programs, maintain daily operations, and oversee financial development of the organization
  • With clear communication skills and decisiveness
  • Who is a values-aligned community member moving with radical liberation politics both inside and outside of their work at PrYSM
  • With a demonstrated commitment to abolitionist practices and to ending cycles of violence in our communities

If this sounds like you, learn more about the position and apply. Reach out to us at info@prysm.us for support. Applications will be accepted on a rolling basis until the position is filled.

August newsletter highlights

Southeast Asian Defense Fund

Since January 2025, ICE has aggressively escalated its violent targeting of immigrant and refugee communities across the United States. Overwhelmingly impacted by these deportations are Southeast Asians — Cambodian, Laotian, Hmong, and Vietnamese folks who resettled in the US and in Rhode Island after being forced to flee political persecution, war, and genocide because of the US wars in Southeast Asia.

PrYSM has been providing deportation defense for Southeast Asian families through mutual aid, legal support, community organizing, and direct action. To continue and expand this work, we are launching the Southeast Asian Defense Fund, a community resource to meet the immediate and long-term needs of those facing detention, deportation, and forced separation.
